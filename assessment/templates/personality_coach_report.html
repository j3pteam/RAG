Reading these results

  • Each everyday trait comes from 3 statements and each pressure tendency from 2, so a single answer can move a score a lot. Look for the themes, not the exact numbers.
  • These are self-ratings: how {{ report.first_name }} sees themselves, not how others see them. The 360 feedback, where available, is the natural cross-check.
  • Original items, not normed and not scientifically validated. Use for development conversations only, never for selection, promotion, or performance decisions.
  • Share what's useful with {{ report.first_name }} in their own development report's language. Avoid quoting the scale labels here (for example "arrogant"), which read as judgments out of context.
